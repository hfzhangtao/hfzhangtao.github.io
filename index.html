About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), specializing in polymer science and additive manufacturing. My research bridges smart materials, sustainable chemistry, and advanced 3D/4D printing technologies.

I received my PhD in Polymer Science from the University of Groningen (RUG) in 2025, where I worked on stimuli-responsive materials and vat photopolymerization. Prior to that, I earned my MSc from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) and my BEng from Dalian University of Technology (DLUT).

With 13 SCI publications and over 900 citations, I am passionate about advancing next-generation intelligent manufacturing and green printing technologies.

Research Interests

4D Printing & Smart Materials

Transition from conventional 3D printing to 4D materials via vat photopolymerization and two-photon polymerization — developing smart materials with dynamic covalent chemistry, preset shape memory, and color-changing effects that respond to external stimuli such as UV light and temperature, expanding their potential in intelligent and adaptive applications.

Sustainable Polymer Inks

Designing and preparing eco-friendly UV-curable polymer inks derived from natural plant-based resources. Optimizing production processes to enhance environmental sustainability, reduce carbon footprint, and improve life cycle assessment.

Multifunctional 3D Printing Materials

Developing multifunctional additive manufacturing materials with antibacterial properties, enhancing their applicability in real-world scenarios while reducing volatile organic compound (VOC) emissions during preparation.

Recyclable Aerogels

Development of closed-loop recyclable polyimide aerogels with sustainable end-of-life management, contributing to circular economy principles in advanced materials.

Flame Retardant Composites & Coatings

Development of flame retardant engineering plastics and coatings (polypropylene, polyurethane, epoxy resin) with enhanced fire safety performance for structural and industrial applications.

Stimuli-Responsive & Luminescent Fibers

Vat photopolymerization of luminescent fibers and stimuli-responsive materials with dynamic optical and mechanical responses for smart textile and sensing applications.

Electroluminescent Materials & Strain Sensors

Development of alternating current electroluminescent (ACEL) materials and ion gel-based resistive strain sensors for flexible electronics, wearable devices, and smart textile applications.
